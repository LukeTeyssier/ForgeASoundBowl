The immersion

Fire, metal and devotion

Forge a Singing Bowl is an immersive experience built around a rare craft: shaping raw brass and bronze, by fire and hammer, into a beautiful singing bowl that is yours to keep.

Each day opens with yoga in the morning. The heart of the day is spent at the forge, in the unique experience created by Resounding Earth. Evenings close with philosophical discussion shared in community and sound baths or participatory live music.

You leave with more than a bowl: you leave with an instrument whose voice you helped bring into the world, along with a transformed heart and spirit.

Is this for you?

Perfect for those who:

  • Long for hands-on making
  • Are drawn to flame and metal
  • Are ripening into their next frequency
  • Are opening to the mysteries of frequency and vibration
  • Are seeking communal ritual
  • Want to weave sound with nature
  • Are ready to go into the deepest places in the self and release more and more light

Daily rhythm

A day in the immersion

  1. Morning

    Yoga and Somatic Practices

    Begin each day grounding body and breath before the work of the forge.

  2. Day

    The forge

    Heat, hammer and shape brass and bronze, step by step, into your own singing bowl.

    Begin with an introduction to metallurgy and forging, with hands-on guidance continuing throughout the week.

  3. Evening

    Community and Music

    Gather for philosophical discussion, then enjoy music and sound bathing.

  4. Outings

    Local Field Trips

    We will offer an outing to local sacred sites and attractions mid-week. At the end of the week, we will play our own singing bowls together at a special location.

Lodging

Quinta Galegas

A traditional country estate in northern Portugal, east of Porto, nestled in the river valley of the Vouga near the village of Ribeiradio.

Quinta Galegas is a lovingly restored 18th-century country house set in five acres of orange groves, olive trees and organic vegetable, herb and flower gardens. It is a peaceful setting of real natural beauty, with a natural lake and swimming pool and views across the valley.

Lodging and meals are included in the immersion.

  • The house

    Bedrooms in the main house, each with its own ensuite, plus a bar, library and shaded courtyard.

  • Poolside rooms

    Three bedrooms in a separate building beside the pool, sharing a bathroom.

  • Garden-fresh food

    Seasonal vegetarian meals inspired by what's growing in the Quinta's own gardens.

    Meat and fish available by advance request. Let us know when you enroll. Eggs served daily with breakfast.

  • The studio

    A large wooden-floored studio on the lower floor of the house for yoga and group practice.

FAQ

Questions

Do I keep the bowl I make?

Yes. The singing bowl you forge during the immersion is your own to take home.

How do I get there?

Porto is the closest airport. From there, you can take a shuttle to Quinta Galegas, about 1.25 hours away.

Will there be an information session?

We are planning 1–2 information sessions before the trip to answer any questions you may have. You are also welcome to reach out using the "Contact" button.

How many participants will there be?

10 participants maximum, plus four facilitators.
