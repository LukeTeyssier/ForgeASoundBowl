The immersion

Fire, metal and devotion

Forge a Sound Bowl is a Bhakti House Immersion built around a rare craft: shaping raw brass and bronze, by fire and by hand, into a beautiful singing bowl that is yours to keep.

Each day opens with yoga in the morning. The heart of the day is spent at the forge, in the unique experience created by Resounding Earth. Evenings close with satsang, philosophical discussion shared in community, and the devotional music of kirtan.

You leave with more than a bowl: you leave with an instrument whose voice you helped bring into the world.

Daily rhythm

A day in the immersion

  1. Morning

    Yoga

    Begin each day on the mat, grounding body and breath before the work of the forge.

  2. Day

    The forge

    Heat, hammer and shape brass and bronze, step by step, into your own singing bowl.

  3. Evening

    Satsang & kirtan

    Gather for philosophical discussion, then let the day resolve into devotional song.

FAQ

Questions

Do I keep the bowl I make?

Yes. The singing bowl you forge during the immersion is your own to take home.

What is satsang?

Satsang means "gathering in truth." It's a time to come together for philosophical discussion, reflection and shared inquiry.

What is kirtan?

Kirtan is devotional call-and-response singing. No musical experience is needed; everyone is welcome to join in.
